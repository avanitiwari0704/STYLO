wardrobe helps you mix-match what you already own — no shopping needed, no cap. free forever, every gender welcome. let's get you set up 💫

1. What we do. wardrobe lets you upload photos of your clothes and get outfit combinations suggested back to you. That's it — no shopping, no selling your stuff.

2. Your photos. Clothing photos you upload are used only to generate your outfit suggestions. We don't share them with anyone else or use them to advertise to you.

3. Your info. Your name, gender, and style preferences are used only to personalise suggestions — never shown publicly to other users.

4. Age. You should be at least 13 years old to use wardrobe. If you're younger, please use it with a parent or guardian.

5. No guarantees. Outfit matches are suggestions based on colour and your preferences — we're not liable if you don't vibe with a suggestion 😅

6. Changes. We may update these terms as the app grows — we'll always let you know.
